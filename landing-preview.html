Problem

New tools fail when habits stay the same.

Most tech projects stall because of people and habits, not bad software. If you do not see how work really moves, agents only speed up the mess.

See the culture before you automate.

What we do

A clear picture first. Agents later.

Tacit maps real communication, tools in use, and company knowledge. That picture lets you change how teams work. After that, we add a judgment layer so agents act like your best people, not empty chatbots.
